Goodminton Tournament

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

This policy covers the Goodminton Tournament web app (the tournament management app for badminton clubs, described at goodminton.club/tournament). It is separate from the Goodminton mobile app privacy policy, which covers a different product. Goodminton Tournament ("we", "the app") is built by Goodminton Club. The app is currently in testing, and this policy describes how it is designed to work.

What we collect

Sign-in

  • Sign in with Google: we ask Google only for the standard openid, email and profile scopes. Google returns your email address, name and profile picture. We use them to create and identify your account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Sign in with an email link: you give us your email address and we send a one-time sign-in link.

Your profile and club information

  • Your name and email address, and optionally a phone number, gender, birth year (used for age-group events), self-reported playing level, affiliation (for example a college or company, for category events), and a profile photo.
  • Your club membership status and roles (for example player, desk staff, or tournament admin). If your club uses a membership roster, the club's organizers may upload a list of member names and member numbers, and your account can be matched against it to confirm you are a member.
  • If a tournament organizer at the desk registers you as a guest, they enter your name, birth year, gender and level, and optionally a contact. A guest added this way has no account.

Tournament activity

  • Your registrations and the events you enter, including partner or team information and team-draw numbers.
  • Levels and ratings, and how they change after matches.
  • Check-in records and your check-in number.
  • Match schedules, scores you or your opponent report, confirmations and disputes, and final results.
  • Payment status as recorded by the desk (paid or unpaid, and the method, such as cash, Zelle or Venmo). See "Payments" below.

Notifications

  • If you choose to turn on web push notifications (for example "your court is called"), we store your browser's push subscription (an endpoint address and encryption keys supplied by your browser's push service, and a short browser description). Notifications are optional and the app asks first. Push messages are delivered through your browser vendor's push service (for example Apple or Google). If you decline, or your browser removes the subscription, we stop sending.

Venue display boards

  • A venue display board (the TV at the tournament) opens an anonymous session. This session has no name or email, is limited to showing one tournament, and expires.

Technical and security records

  • Audit log: organizers' and players' actions that change tournament data (for example registering, entering a score, or changing a draw) are recorded so disputes can be resolved. The log records who did what and when, and refers to players by internal reference rather than by name where practical.
  • Error reports: when the app hits an error, it can send a short report (app version, error code, short message, and a page template without personal identifiers). These are kept for 30 days.
  • Rate-limit records: short-lived records (about 2 days) used to stop abuse, such as repeated check-in or membership-check attempts, which may be tied to your account or network address.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the app, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

Who can see your information

  • Public venue board: the board shown at the venue is viewable by anyone who has the board's link. It shows first name and last initial, court, event, round, scores, the call queue and recent results. It does not show birth year, gender, contact details, profile photos, membership or ratings. A junior's guardian can choose to have the junior shown by entry number only.
  • Signed-in users: once an organizer publishes a draw, signed-in users of the app can see that draw's bracket and match results. Your profile details, registration, ratings, check-in and payment status are visible only to you and to the organizers and desk staff of the relevant tournament or club.
  • Organizers: tournament owners, admins and desk staff can see the registration, contact, check-in and payment information needed to run their event.
  • Profile photos are stored privately and are shown only to signed-in viewers within the same tournament; they never appear on public surfaces.

Payments

The app does not process card or online payments. Entry fees for a tournament are handled by the club at the desk (for example cash, Zelle or Venmo), and the desk records whether an entry is paid. The app stores that status and method only.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data for advertising.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account and tournament history while your account exists, so brackets, results and ratings stay accurate.
  • Deleting your account: the app is designed to let you delete your account. When you do, your name, contact details, birth year, profile photo and member number are removed or replaced with placeholders ("Deleted Player"), your push subscriptions are deleted, and your sign-in account is deleted. Match results stay attached to the anonymized player so other players' brackets remain intact. Deletion may be blocked while you are the last owner of a tournament that is not finished.
  • To request deletion or a copy of your data (or if the in-app option is not yet available), email kevinliu217@gmail.com from your account email address. We aim to respond within 30 days.
  • Short-lived technical records are removed automatically: error reports after 30 days and rate-limit records after about 2 days. The audit log is kept as an append-only record and refers to players by internal reference. Encrypted backups may contain older copies of data until they are rotated out.

Children

Goodminton Tournament is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without a parent or guardian. Juniors (under 18) can take part in club tournaments through a parent or guardian: a guardian can manage a junior's profile and gives consent before the junior is accepted into an event. Juniors' profile photos, birth years and genders are never shown on public surfaces. If you believe a child has given us personal data without a guardian's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when this policy changes. If a change is material, we will tell signed-in users in the app.
