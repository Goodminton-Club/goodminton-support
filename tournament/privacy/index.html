Goodminton Tournament

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

This policy covers the Goodminton Tournament web app (the tournament management app for badminton clubs, described at goodminton.club/tournament). It is separate from the Goodminton mobile app privacy policy, which covers a different product. Goodminton Tournament ("we", "the app") is built by Goodminton Club. The app is currently in testing, and this policy describes how it is designed to work.

What we collect

Sign-in

  • Sign in with Google: we ask Google only for the standard openid, email and profile scopes. Google returns your email address, name and profile picture. We use them to create and identify your account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Sign in with an email link: you give us your email address and we send a one-time sign-in link.

Your profile and club information

  • Your name and email address, and optionally a phone number, gender, birth year (used for age-group events), self-reported playing level, affiliation (for example a college or company, for category events), and a profile photo.
  • A parent or guardian can create and manage a profile for a junior, and records consent for the junior to take part.
  • Your club membership status and roles (for example player, desk staff, or tournament admin). If your club uses a membership roster, the club's organizers may upload a list of members (name, member number, membership name, status and expiry date), including people who never use the app. When you claim a membership, we store the name you typed, the member number and the outcome of the check.
  • If a tournament organizer at the desk registers you as a guest, they enter your name, birth year, gender and level, and optionally a contact. A guest added this way has no account.

Tournament activity

  • Your registrations and the events you enter, including partner or team information and team-draw numbers, and any "ready now, skip rest" choice you make for your next match.
  • Levels and ratings, and how they change after matches.
  • Check-in records (time and method) and your check-in number.
  • Match schedules, scores you or your opponent report, confirmations and disputes, and final results.
  • Payment status (paid or unpaid) and, for entries the desk takes payment for, the method (cash, Zelle or Venmo). See "Payments" below.
  • In-app notifications sent to you (type, content and whether you read them).

Invitations

  • When a captain invites a partner or teammate, the captain enters the invitee's email address. The invitation link only works for an account signed in with that verified email address (or for that person's guardian). The invitee's email address is visible only to the captain and to the tournament's organizers and desk staff. It is never shown to anyone else and is not written to logs. It is deleted at most one day after the invitation ends, that is, when it is accepted, cancelled or declined, or expires after 7 days.

Notifications

  • If you choose to turn on web push notifications (for example "your court is called"), we store your browser's push subscription (an endpoint address and encryption keys supplied by your browser's push service, and a short browser description). Notifications are optional and the app asks first. Push messages are delivered through your browser vendor's push service (for example Apple or Google). If you decline, or your browser removes the subscription, we stop sending.

Venue display boards

  • A venue display board (the TV at the tournament) opens an anonymous session. This session has no name or email, is limited to showing one tournament, and expires after 24 hours. Our sign-in service keeps an anonymous account record for it with no personal details.

Technical and security records

  • Audit log: actions that change tournament data (for example registering, entering a score, or changing a draw) are recorded so disputes can be resolved, and so that a tournament day can be replayed for testing. The log records who did what and when. Audit records are designed to contain references (internal player and entry identifiers) rather than personal details such as names, contact details or birth year. The audit log is append-only and is kept indefinitely.
  • Error reports: when the app hits an error, it can send a short report (app version, error code, short message, and a page template without personal identifiers). These are kept for 30 days.
  • Rate-limit records: short-lived records (about 2 days) used to stop abuse, such as repeated check-in or membership-check attempts, which may be tied to your account or network address.
  • Request records: to make retries safe, the app briefly stores the responses to your actions (7 days). Our sign-in service (Supabase Auth) also logs sign-in activity, including IP address and browser details.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the app, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

Who can see your information

  • Public venue board: the board shown at the venue is viewable by anyone who has the board's link. It shows first name and last initial, court, event, round, scores, the call queue (with each player's check-in number) and recent results. It does not show birth year, gender, contact details, profile photos, membership or ratings. You (or a junior's guardian) can choose to be shown by entry number only.
  • Other signed-in users: any signed-in user of the app can see the entry list of any tournament that is open for registration or later (each entrant's first name and last initial, or entry number, per event), matches that are not part of a draw, team line-ups once locked, and the brackets, groups, schedules and results once a draw is published. Members of a club can also see that club's ladder standings. Other players cannot see your contact details, birth year, gender, profile photo, ratings or payment status.
  • You and your guardian: you can see your own profile, registrations, ratings, check-ins and payments. A junior's guardian can see the junior's data.
  • Organizers: a club's owners, admins and desk staff, and a tournament's admins, desk staff and umpires, can see the profile, contact details, birth year, registration, check-in and payment information of the players in their club or tournament, as needed to run the event.
  • Platform administrators: Goodminton's platform administrators, who operate the service, can access all data for support, security and operations.
  • Profile photos are stored privately. They are visible only to you, a junior's guardian, and the organizers, desk staff and umpires of tournaments you enter or clubs you belong to. They never appear on the public board and are not shown to other players.

Payments

The app does not process card or online payments. Entry fees are handled by the club outside the app (for example at the desk by cash, Zelle or Venmo). The app stores only whether an entry is paid and, for desk-paid entries, the method.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data for advertising.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account and tournament history while your account exists, so brackets, results and ratings stay accurate.
  • Deleting your account: the app is designed to let you delete your account. When you do:
    • Cleared or replaced: your names (replaced with the placeholder "Deleted Player"), email address, phone number, birth year, profile photo, member number and affiliation. The profile photo file itself is removed from storage, and staff review notes about you are cleared. Your club memberships are retired, your push subscriptions are removed, and your sign-in account is deleted.
    • Kept, on the anonymized record: gender, playing level, ratings and rating history, check-in records, score-report notes, and match results, so other players' brackets and results remain intact. Team names are also kept, because they are shared with your partners or teammates.
    If you are the last owner of a club, you must transfer ownership before deleting your account.
  • To request deletion or a copy of your data (or if the in-app option is not yet available), email kevinliu217@gmail.com from your account email address. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Retention periods: rows of a superseded club roster import are deleted 30 days after a newer import replaces them (only the current roster is kept); error reports 30 days; rate-limit records about 2 days; request records 7 days; encrypted database backups up to 30 days, so deleted database records can remain in backups for at most 30 days (profile photo image files are not backed up, only database records, so a lost photo would need to be uploaded again); the audit log is kept indefinitely (it is designed to contain internal references rather than personal details, and is not deleted when an account is deleted).

Children

Goodminton Tournament is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without a parent or guardian. Juniors (under 18) can take part in club tournaments through a parent or guardian: a guardian can create and manage a junior's profile and gives consent for the junior to take part. Juniors' profile photos, birth years and genders are never shown on public surfaces. If a child under 13 has registered an account themselves, we will delete their data once we learn of it. If you believe this has happened, contact us.

Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when this policy changes. We will notify you in the app or by email before material changes take effect.
