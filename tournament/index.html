Goodminton Tournament

Badminton tournament management for clubs

Goodminton Tournament is a web app that helps badminton clubs run their tournaments, from sign-up to final results. It is built by Goodminton Club and runs in your phone's browser; there is nothing to install from an app store.

What it does

  • Registration: players sign in and enter the events they want to play; clubs set a registration deadline.
  • Draws: organizers build brackets, round-robin groups and team draws.
  • Check-in with QR: players check in by scanning a QR code shown on the venue's display board, or at the desk.
  • Live scoring and board: players report scores from their phones, and a venue display board shows courts, the call queue and recent results.
  • Results: brackets, standings and match history after the event.

Status

Coming soon. Goodminton Tournament is currently in testing and is not yet open to the public. Features and availability may change.

Sign-in

You can sign in with Google or with an email link. The app asks Google only for your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) so it can create your account. See the Goodminton Tournament Privacy Policy for details.
